PART I — DECISION

1 · RECOMMENDATION

✔ PROCEED — final bid of $715MM enterprise value, fixed, submitted 21 Aug 2026

12.2x the corrected $58.5MM base and 10.5x the seller's marketing number. Structure per §12: $292.5MM Halcyon Ridge Credit unitranche at 5.0x, $118.0MM founder rollover (25.9% of the equity), $335.5MM Fund V equity. Organic base of 2.46x / 19.7% with the value-creation program at §9 delivered; platform M&A adds an estimated +0.4–0.5x and is deliberately not underwritten. The bid may flex to $765MM only against full exclusivity, the Mireille Solenne rollover executed at signing, and the Luminelle JBP letter in hand — the three things that de-risk the two dated events in the downside calendar.

✘ DECLINE — above $765MM, and at the $920–985MM guidance frame at any structure

Above $765MM the base case falls through 2.2x and the price begins to underwrite the seller's plan rather than the diligence record. At the frame, three facts are dispositive: (1) our base returns 1.61x; (2) the seller's plan delivered in full returns 2.43x — still below target; (3) the downside returns 0.62x. A price that needs the best case to reach the target return has no owner of the downside. If the process clears above $765MM, the correct outcome is to lose.

Why this is the frame's problem and not ours

Halloran & Reed built the guidance on the $68.0MM marketing base and a precedent set drawn from scaled, internationally proven platforms (§11). SBG is neither yet — it is a superb domestic asset with one hero brand mid-flight. Paying the scaled-platform multiple on the pre-scale asset hands the seller the value-creation plan Windrose would spend five years delivering. At $715MM, the sponsor is paid to build; at $950MM, the sponsor pays for the building it has not yet done.

3 · THE THREE EQUITY QUESTIONS — ANSWERED

Every consumer deal turns on three questions. Here they are answered with the evidence, and each answer prices directly into the bid.

Q1 — Is the growth the consumer's or the channel's? Mostly the consumer's: +6.6pts of the +9.4pts is verified pull — same-door velocity +6.1%, price realization holding, DTC repeat above 41%, branded search +21%. The remaining +5.1pts is Luminelle/Fairgate door fill that completes by mid-FY27 and does not repeat. The base case carries the pull and retires the fill — which is why our FY27 growth is +7.8% against a seller plan of +14%.

The gap between those two assumptions is worth roughly $145MM of price at the guidance multiple — the entire argument of this memo in one row.

Q2 — Is the 60.6% gross margin structural or promotional? Structural, with a working capital and mix caveat. Margin has expanded 250bps over three years on mix (Solenne up) and price, not on under-spending: trade is rising 90bps/yr and the QoE strips the marketing that was hiding in addbacks. The FY26→FY31 bridge at §9 is +330bps gross of a −120bps mass-mix headwind — every lever named, owned and dated. The margin risk that matters is not gross margin; it is trade-spend defense in the downside, and it is modeled at §8.

Q3 — Who buys this from us, and at what number? Both strategics and sponsors — if we build the two things the current asset lacks: revenue scale past ~$400MM and international proof. The precedent set at §11 splits cleanly: scaled, internationally proven platforms cleared 12.8–14.5x; domestic hero-brand assets cleared 10.0–11.2x. Our base exits at 11.75x — the 45th percentile of the set, earned by FY29 scale, not assumed. The exit thesis is the value-creation plan; they are the same document.

The read

The underwriting base is $58.5MM — 20.5% of revenue — with a documented, contingent path to $60.3MM. The seller's $68.0MM is an honest number honestly assembled under a different standard: it capitalizes marketing the business cannot stop buying and books deductions on a lag the ledger has already caught up with. Fairhaven Advisory's Phase II work closed the three items left open at the preliminary read — all three landed on our side of the line. Nothing in this section argues the brand is weaker than presented; it argues that $9.5MM of the presented margin belongs to the operating model, not to earnings.

20 · METHODOLOGY & ENGAGEMENT STANDARD

The standard: every figure in this package is one of four things — verified (tied to a native source: file, tab, cell), seller (their claim, displayed and labeled, never adopted), external (purchased or fielded outside the room, used for triangulation only), or model (our construction, assumptions stated where used). Tags travel with the numbers. Where evidence was absent, the package says so and prices the gap — a flagged gap beats a guess, and both beat a blended average of the two.

Independence: seller claims were tested, never averaged. The two places the record improved on the seller's story — the freight credit at §4.2 and the inventory-band confirmation at §3 — are displayed with the same weight as the nine places it did not. That symmetry is the product.
